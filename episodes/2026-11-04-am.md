🇿🇦 Your 60-second SA news update for Wednesday, 4 November.

🗳️ South Africa votes today in the local government elections. About 29 million people are registered, and voting stations are open from 7am until 9pm.
⛈️ Severe thunderstorms (Level 4 warning) are expected in parts of Mpumalanga and north-western KwaZulu-Natal this afternoon, with extreme heat in the Western and Northern Cape.
⛽ New fuel prices kick in today: diesel drops by more than R1 a litre, petrol falls by just 1 cent, and paraffin goes up sharply.
🏛️ Parliament has announced the sudden death of NCOP Secretary Modibedi Phindela, who held the post since 2010.
🥇 In Dakar, 16-year-old rower Callum Dreyer has won Team SA's first gold at the Youth Olympics, in the coastal beach sprint.
